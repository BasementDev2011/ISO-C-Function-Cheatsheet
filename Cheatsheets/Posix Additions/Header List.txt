Headers with POSIX additions:

- ctype.h    (e.g. isascii, toascii, and the _l locale-variant functions)
- errno.h    (additional POSIX-defined error codes)
- limits.h   (additional POSIX-defined limits, e.g. PATH_MAX, NAME_MAX)
- locale.h   (newlocale, uselocale, freelocale, duplocale, LC_GLOBAL_LOCALE)
- math.h     (Bessel functions j0/j1/jn/y0/y1/yn, lgamma_r, etc.)
- setjmp.h   (sigsetjmp, siglongjmp)
- signal.h   (sigaction, sigprocmask, sigemptyset/sigfillset/sigaddset/etc.,
              kill, sigsuspend, sigaltstack, pthread_sigmask, sigwait, ...)
- stdio.h    (fileno, fdopen, popen/pclose, getline, getdelim, dprintf,
              flockfile/funlockfile, fmemopen, open_memstream, ...)
- stdlib.h   (mkstemp, realpath, setenv/unsetenv, random/srandom family,
              drand48 family, posix_openpt, getsubopt, ...)
- string.h   (strdup, strndup, strtok_r, strerror_r, strcasecmp,
              strncasecmp, ffs, memccpy, stpcpy, stpncpy)
- time.h     (gmtime_r, localtime_r, asctime_r, ctime_r, strptime,
              nanosleep, clock_gettime family, timer_* family, tzset, ...)

Headers with NO (or negligible) POSIX additions:

- assert.h
- float.h
- stdarg.h
- stddef.h

This folder is a work in progress.
